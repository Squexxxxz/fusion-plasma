No extra photos are required. Most valuable optional additions:
1. One new landscape machine photograph, at least 2000 pixels wide. Entire device and handpiece in frame, neutral charcoal or pale background, even light, no people reflected on screen, spare space around edges.
2. One matching detail photograph, at least 1200 pixels, square or portrait. Sharp handpiece and actual included tip, same lighting/background.
3. Replace existing comparison files with their original full-resolution pairs if available; do not add arbitrary extra cases. Matching light, angle, framing, and documented dates/session counts improve interpretability. Preserve consent and permission records.

All public image conversions use WebP for faster loading. Original comparison pairs remain intact; no retouching, fabricated results, or image generation was used.
